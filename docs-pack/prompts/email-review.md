# Email Review Prompt

Review a proposed outbound email.

Check:
1. recipient correctness;
2. email verification;
3. greeting correctness;
4. factual accuracy;
5. evidence coverage;
6. target relevance;
7. clarity;
8. length;
9. tone;
10. CTA;
11. attachment appropriateness;
12. duplicate/contact history;
13. Covey/Voss quality;
14. privacy/security.

Return PASS/FAIL plus issues and suggested corrections.

Do not silently rewrite unsupported claims.
