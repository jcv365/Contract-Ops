# Real Data Quality Gates

## Opportunity ACTIONABLE
Requires known source/URL, successful retrieval, evidence of current/open status, contract status, remote status, hard-filter evaluation and evidence-backed fit score. Otherwise REVIEW, EXCLUDED or UNKNOWN.

## Recruiter EMAIL_READY
Requires verified company, verified recruiter identity when named, and an email directly supported by reliable evidence or a verification provider with timestamp.

## Company OUTREACH_READY
Requires verified company, evidence-backed reason for targeting, selected proposition and valid contact channel.

## Rate
Requires numeric value, currency, period or explicit unknown, and source. Preserve original rate. ZAR conversion never replaces it.

## Conflicts
If sources disagree, preserve both, mark CONFLICTED and require explicit precedence or human review.
