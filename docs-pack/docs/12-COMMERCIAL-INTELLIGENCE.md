# Commercial Intelligence

## Rate handling

Always preserve:
- original rate;
- original currency;
- period;
- source;
- timestamp.

Calculate:
- ZAR rate;
- monthly equivalent where sensible;
- total contract estimate where duration is known.

FX must be timestamped and sourced.

## Benchmarking

Where sufficient data exists, compare:
- role;
- geography;
- technology;
- seniority;
- contract duration;
- remote model.

Do not present a benchmark as fact when sample size is insufficient.

## Negotiation

Produce:
- target;
- reasonable range;
- walk-away recommendation;
- rationale;
- negotiation risks.

Do not make financial/legal guarantees.
