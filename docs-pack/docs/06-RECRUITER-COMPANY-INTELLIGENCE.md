# Recruiter and Company Intelligence

## Recruiter discovery

Find recruiters who place:
- Azure;
- cloud;
- enterprise architecture;
- infrastructure;
- hybrid cloud;
- FinOps;
- AI/platform;
- networking/data centre;
- DevOps;
- transformation professionals.

Search globally.

Prioritise recruiters with evidence of:
- contract/freelance placements;
- remote roles;
- international placement;
- relevant technologies;
- senior architecture roles.

## Recruiter record

Store:
- company;
- name;
- title;
- specialism;
- country/region;
- LinkedIn/public profile;
- email;
- email type;
- verification source;
- verification date;
- source URL;
- relationship status;
- last contact;
- response history;
- opportunity history.

## Email rule

If named email independently verified:
- address person by first name.

If only verified generic/team/company email:
- address company/team.

If email is inferred/guessed/unverified:
- do not send;
- do not present as email-ready.

## Professional company discovery

Find:
- cloud consultancies;
- systems integrators;
- professional services firms;
- specialist technology consultancies;
- managed services firms;
- delivery partners;
- companies likely to subcontract specialist architects.

Score:
- cloud relevance;
- Azure relevance;
- contract usage;
- remote delivery;
- international work;
- subcontracting potential;
- company size/market;
- current transformation activity.

## Contact roles

Potential contacts:
- practice director;
- cloud practice lead;
- delivery director;
- resource manager;
- talent acquisition;
- partner;
- programme director;
- head of cloud;
- enterprise architecture lead;
- procurement.

Never infer an email address from the person's name.
