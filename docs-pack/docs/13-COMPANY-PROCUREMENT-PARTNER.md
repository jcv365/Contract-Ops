# Company, Procurement and Partner Intelligence

## Direct company intelligence

Find organisations with evidence of:
- cloud transformation;
- Azure adoption;
- enterprise platform work;
- infrastructure modernisation;
- AI/cloud programmes;
- external consultant usage.

## Partner intelligence

Identify professional firms/SIs that may subcontract:
- Azure architects;
- enterprise architects;
- cloud transformation specialists;
- infrastructure architects;
- networking/data-centre specialists;
- FinOps specialists.

## Procurement intelligence

Where accessible, find:
- tenders;
- frameworks;
- RFPs;
- RFIs;
- ITTs;
- supplier panels;
- cloud consulting procurements.

Record:
- buyer;
- procurement portal;
- closing date;
- scope;
- eligibility;
- framework duration;
- supplier requirements;
- source URL.

## Phase 1 rule

These are discovery and intelligence features, not autonomous bid submission.

Human approval is required for any external response.
